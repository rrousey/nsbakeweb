Our Story

North Star Bakery was launched in 2010 by a pair of traveling nomadic bakers who learned secret baking techniques from a collective of mysterious monks! From the temple to your kitchen! Fresh every day.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts with great ingredients. We work with local mills and farms whenever possible, choosing organic flour, seasonal fruit, and dairy from nearby producers who are members of our own community!
